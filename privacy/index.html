CardRadar Collector Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

CardRadar Collector ("CardRadar", "we", "our", or "the app") is a sports card collection application that allows users to scan, identify, organize, value, and manage their sports card collections.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use CardRadar Collector and how that information is used.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, CardRadar Collector may process:

• Email address
• Firebase user identifier (UID)
• Account plan status, such as Free or Premium
• Account creation and authentication information

Authentication services are provided through Google Firebase Authentication.

Collection Information

When you use the app, we may store information associated with your sports card collection, including:

• Player name
• Card series or set
• Card type
• Card numbering
• Estimated or entered card value
• Marketplace listing references
• Image URLs
• Other card-related information entered or generated through the app

Collection data is associated with your authenticated account.

Camera and Photos

CardRadar Collector may request access to:

• Your device camera, to scan sports cards
• Your photo library, when you choose to select a card image

Camera and photo access is used only for functionality you initiate.

Photos selected manually may remain stored locally on your device unless a cloud image storage feature is specifically enabled in a future version.

Scanner and Card Recognition

The app may process images or recognized text in order to identify card information and find possible matches.

Marketplace and Pricing Information

CardRadar Collector may retrieve publicly available card marketplace information from third-party services, including eBay-related listings and pricing information.

CardRadar Collector is not affiliated with or endorsed by eBay.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by CardRadar Collector may be used to:

• Create and authenticate your account
• Synchronize your collection between devices
• Display your saved cards
• Identify and match sports cards
• Provide card pricing and marketplace information
• Apply Free and Premium account limits
• Maintain and improve app functionality
• Protect user accounts and data

3. Cloud Services

CardRadar Collector currently uses Google Firebase services, including:

• Firebase Authentication
• Cloud Firestore

Cloud Firestore may store your account plan and card collection data.

Firebase services are operated by Google and may process information according to Google's own privacy policies.

4. Data Separation Between Users

Each authenticated CardRadar Collector account has a unique Firebase UID.

Collection data stored in Cloud Firestore is separated by user account. Security rules are used so authenticated users can access only data associated with their own UID.

5. Local Storage

The app may store cached collection information on your device.

Local collection storage is separated by authenticated user account.

Local data may remain on a device until the application data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

6. Free and Premium Accounts

CardRadar Collector may provide Free and Premium account tiers.

Account plan status may be stored in association with your user account.

Future versions may offer subscriptions or purchases through Apple App Store and Google Play. Additional purchase-related information may then be processed by Apple, Google, or other payment providers according to their own policies.

7. Third-Party Services

CardRadar Collector may rely on third-party services including:

• Google Firebase
• Apple services
• Google Play services
• eBay or marketplace-related services

These third parties may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures intended to protect user data, including authenticated access and database security rules.

No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

CardRadar Collector is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us.

10. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Account and collection information may be retained while your CardRadar Collector account remains active.

Users may request deletion of their account and associated cloud data.

An in-app account deletion option may also be provided before public release where required by platform rules.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when features, services, or legal requirements change.

The latest version will display its effective date at the top of this document.
