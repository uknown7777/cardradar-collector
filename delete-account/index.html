Delete Your Account

CardRadar Collector allows registered users to permanently delete their account and associated account data.

Delete your account from the app

To permanently delete your account:

  1. Open CardRadar Collector.
  2. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
  3. Open the Profile page.
  4. Tap DELETE ACCOUNT.
  5. Confirm the deletion.

After confirmation, your CardRadar Collector account and associated cloud collection data are permanently deleted.

What is deleted

  • Your CardRadar Collector authentication account.
  • Your cloud-saved card collection associated with that account.
  • Your CardRadar Collector user record associated with the account.
  • Locally cached collection data associated with that account on the device.

Important

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. After deletion, you will need to create a new account if you want to use CardRadar Collector with an account again.
