# CardRadar Collector Support

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Welcome to CardRadar Collector Support.

CardRadar Collector helps users scan, identify, organize, value, and manage sports card collections.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I create an account?

Open CardRadar Collector and select Create Account.

Enter your email address and password. You may be asked to verify your email before using the app.

### I forgot my password. What should I do?

On the login screen, select:

FORGOT PASSWORD?

Enter your account email address and CardRadar Collector will send you a password reset email.

### Why do I need to verify my email?

Email verification helps protect your account and confirms that you have access to the email address used to register.

### Are my cards synchronized between devices?

When you sign in with the same CardRadar Collector account, supported collection information is synchronized through cloud services.

Each user account has its own separate collection.

### Are manually selected card photos synchronized?

Manual photos stored only on your device may not currently synchronize between devices.

Marketplace image URLs and supported cloud collection information may synchronize where available.

### What are the Free plan limits?

The Free plan currently includes:

- Up to 2 card scans per day
- Up to 5 cards in your Collection
- Find Card Matches
- Manual card entry
- Card prices in EUR

### What does Premium include?

Premium is intended to include features such as:

- Unlimited card scans
- Unlimited Collection
- Find Card Matches
- Market Price
- eBay Active Listings
- Prices in EUR

Additional features may be introduced in future updates.

### Why is a card match incorrect?

CardRadar Collector uses automated card recognition and marketplace matching.

Results may not always identify the exact card, parallel, numbering, variation, or condition.

Always review the detected information before saving a card.

### Are market prices guaranteed?

No.

Market prices and marketplace listings are provided for informational purposes only and may change at any time.

CardRadar Collector does not guarantee that a card can be bought or sold at a displayed price.

### Why is a marketplace listing unavailable?

Third-party marketplace listings can be changed, sold, removed, or expire at any time.

CardRadar Collector does not control third-party marketplaces.

### Can I delete my account?

Users may request deletion of their CardRadar Collector account and associated cloud data.

An in-app account deletion option may also be provided before public release where required by platform rules.

For account deletion requests, contact support using the email below.
